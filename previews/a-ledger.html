Named for the one who kept the records.

Seshat was the Egyptian goddess of writing and measure. She kept the accounts, and she marked out the plan before a temple went up.

We borrowed her name and her habits: write things down, measure first, and build what lasts. That is the whole of it. You will not find pyramids on our products.
